# Contributor Licence Agreement

> **This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** It is adapted from the
> Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, which
> is widely used and widely understood, with one substantive change: section 2
> adds an explicit right to relicense, which the Apache original does not need
> and dual-licensed projects do. Have a lawyer review it before relying on it
> commercially. The maintainers are not offering legal advice by publishing it.

## Why this exists

Mezzanine is dual-licensed: AGPL v3 for everyone, and a
[commercial licence](COMMERCIAL.md) for organisations that cannot accept the
AGPL. Offering both requires holding rights broad enough to grant both.

If you contribute code and grant nothing beyond the AGPL, that code can only
ever be distributed under the AGPL. One such contribution anywhere in the
tree makes a commercial licence covering the whole project impossible to
offer honestly. This agreement is what avoids that.

It is **not** a copyright assignment. You keep ownership of everything you
write, and you keep the right to use your own contribution however you like,
including in other projects. What you grant is a licence — broad, but a
licence.

## How to sign

Add a `Signed-off-by:` line to your commits certifying that you have read and
agree to this document:

```
git commit -s
```

and say in your first pull request that you accept the CLA. For a corporate
contribution — anything written in the course of employment — see section 4
before you open the PR.
